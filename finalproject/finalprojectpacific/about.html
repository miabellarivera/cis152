Our Team

The Staff at Pacific Trails are dedicated at providing a memorable experience for all guests. We are a team that shares a love for the California coast and everything it has to offer. We want our guests to feel welcome and enjoy their time with us, whether they are hiking along the scenic trails or exploring the beautiful coastline. Our goal is to make every visit enjoyable and give our guests a place where they can relax, have fun, and create lasting memories. We have a friendly and welcoming team that is always ready to help. We are passionate about the Pacific Trails and we want to make sure our guests feel like they are in good hands throughout their stay.
